APEX GAMES

Community Guidelines

Respect other players

Harassment, threats, targeted abuse, and hateful behavior do not belong in the Apex Games community.

Keep personal information private

Do not post private or identifying information about yourself or another person.

Don't spam or scam

Avoid repeated unwanted posts, deceptive links, impersonation, and attempts to trick other players.

Keep content appropriate

Community content should be appropriate for the Apex Games audience and follow platform rules.

Report problems

When reporting and blocking tools are available, use them when content or another account violates community rules.
